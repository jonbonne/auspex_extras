The Super Nintendo Entertainment System (SNES) supports multiple background modes, each with different color depths and graphical capabilities. These background modes are often referred to as "BG Modes," and they range from Mode 0 to Mode 7. Here's a breakdown of the color depths for the various SNES background modes:

### SNES Background Modes and Color Depths:

1. **Mode 0**
   - **Backgrounds**: 4 backgrounds
   - **Color Depth**: 2 bits per pixel (4 colors) for each background
   - **Total Colors**: Each background can use 4 colors from a palette of 16 colors per background, allowing for up to 16 colors total across all backgrounds.

2. **Mode 1**
   - **Backgrounds**: 3 backgrounds
   - **Color Depth**:
     - BG1 and BG2: 4 bits per pixel (16 colors)
     - BG3: 2 bits per pixel (4 colors)
   - **Total Colors**: 16 colors for BG1 and BG2, 4 colors for BG3. Up to 32 colors total.

3. **Mode 2**
   - **Backgrounds**: 2 backgrounds
   - **Color Depth**: 4 bits per pixel (16 colors) for each background
   - **Total Colors**: Up to 32 colors total.

4. **Mode 3**
   - **Backgrounds**: 2 backgrounds
   - **Color Depth**:
     - BG1: 8 bits per pixel (256 colors)
     - BG2: 4 bits per pixel (16 colors)
   - **Total Colors**: 256 colors for BG1 and 16 colors for BG2. Up to 272 colors total.

5. **Mode 4**
   - **Backgrounds**: 2 backgrounds
   - **Color Depth**:
     - BG1: 8 bits per pixel (256 colors)
     - BG2: 2 bits per pixel (4 colors)
   - **Total Colors**: 256 colors for BG1 and 4 colors for BG2. Up to 260 colors total.

6. **Mode 5**
   - **Backgrounds**: 2 backgrounds
   - **Color Depth**:
     - BG1: 4 bits per pixel (16 colors)
     - BG2: 2 bits per pixel (4 colors)
   - **Total Colors**: Up to 20 colors total.
   - **Note**: Mode 5 uses a higher resolution (512×448/512×480 pixels), but this is at the cost of color depth.

7. **Mode 6**
   - **Backgrounds**: 1 background
   - **Color Depth**: 4 bits per pixel (16 colors)
   - **Total Colors**: Up to 16 colors total.
   - **Note**: Mode 6 is similar to Mode 5 but with only one background, allowing for special effects like screen flipping.

8. **Mode 7**
   - **Backgrounds**: 1 background
   - **Color Depth**: 8 bits per pixel (256 colors)
   - **Total Colors**: 256 colors.
   - **Note**: Mode 7 is famous for its ability to rotate and scale backgrounds, often used for special effects like the rotating floor in "Super Mario Kart" and the world map in "Final Fantasy VI."

### Summary of Color Depths:
- **2 bits per pixel (4 colors)**: Modes 0, 1 (BG3), and 4 (BG2)
- **4 bits per pixel (16 colors)**: Modes 0, 1 (BG1 and BG2), 2, 3 (BG2), 5 (BG1), and 6
- **8 bits per pixel (256 colors)**: Modes 3 (BG1), 4 (BG1), and 7

Each mode has different uses depending on the number of backgrounds, color depth, and special effects required by the game. The SNES's flexibility in these modes allowed developers to create visually varied and complex games.
