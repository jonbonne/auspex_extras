suggesstions: 
Bebas Neue
A tall, condensed sans-serif with great impact. Works beautifully for headlines and alerts.

Montserrat
A versatile, geometric sans-serif with multiple weights. It’s neutral but still feels fresh and professional.

Anton
Similar to Bebas Neue but even wider—perfect for big, bold overlays (think “LIVE,” “NEW SUB,” etc.).

Oswald
A reworking of the classic “Alternate Gothic” style. Has a bit more character and works well for titles and labels.

Roboto
Google’s neo-grotesque sans-serif: very legible at small and large sizes, with a friendly, open feel.

League Spartan
A strong, all-caps display face with sturdy letterforms—great for channel branding and lower-thirds.

Exo 2
Tech-inspired shapes with subtle curves. Feels futuristic without being too gimmicky.

Press Start 2P
An 8-bit, pixel-style font that’s perfect if you’re going for a retro gaming look.

VT323
A monospaced font that emulates old CRT‐terminal text—awesome for “hacker” or “console” themed streams.

Raleway
An elegant, thin sans-serif that’s great for secondary text, captions, or any overlay where you don’t want to overpower the video.
